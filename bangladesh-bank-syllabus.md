**Posts covered:** Senior Officer (General), Officer (General), and Officer (Cash)  
**Exam stages:** MCQ preliminary → written examination → viva (as specified in the recruitment notice)

> **Please check your job circular:** The syllabus and marks distribution can change from one recruitment cycle to another. The breakdown below reflects the reported 2026 pattern. The notice for your specific post and job ID is the final authority.

## What to prepare

- **Bangla:** grammar, vocabulary, sentence correction and writing practice.
- **English:** grammar, vocabulary, sentence correction, comprehension, précis and essay writing as required by the post.
- **Mathematics:** SSC-level arithmetic and quantitative problem solving; practise for both speed and accuracy.
- **Analytical skills:** logical reasoning and problem solving (a separate MCQ section in the reported Senior Officer pattern).
- **General knowledge:** Bangladesh affairs, international affairs, and current events.
- **Banking and finance:** core banking and financial concepts; a separate MCQ and written section appears in the reported Senior Officer pattern.
- **ICT / computer:** basic computer and information and communication technology concepts.
- **Written practice:** timed focus writing, translation, comprehension and any post-specific reasoning or argumentative writing.

# Other Bangladesh job exams

Unlike the combined bank recruitment exams, **private banks, Petrobangla, BEPZA, and other public or autonomous organizations do not share one fixed syllabus**. Their exam format depends on the employer, post, grade, and recruitment circular. Use the outlines below as preparation guides, then follow the specific circular and admit-card instructions.

## Private commercial banks

Private banks may recruit through an aptitude/MCQ test, written exam, interview, or a combination. There is no single syllabus used by every bank. Bangladesh Bank issued a circular in September 2026 requiring entry-level private-bank hiring to include objective or written assessment and an oral examination; check the hiring bank's current instructions for the actual process.

### Common preparation areas

- **English:** grammar, vocabulary, sentence correction, reading comprehension, précis, email or business writing, and sometimes essays.
- **Mathematics and quantitative aptitude:** percentages, ratios, profit and loss, interest, averages, time and work, speed and distance, data interpretation, and basic statistics.
- **Analytical and logical reasoning:** series, patterns, arrangements, deductions, and data sufficiency.
- **Bangla:** grammar and comprehension, when included.
- **General knowledge and current affairs:** Bangladesh, international affairs, economy, and financial news.
- **Banking and finance:** basic banking products, central-bank functions, monetary policy, financial terms, and customer service.
- **Computer skills:** office applications and basic ICT, if required.
- **Written tasks/interview:** communication, situational judgment, role knowledge, and questions about the bank and your own background.

The balance differs by role. An MTO or general banking role may emphasize aptitude and communication; IT, credit, audit, or treasury roles may include more technical questions.

## Petrobangla and its companies

Petrobangla and its companies recruit for both **general/administrative** and **technical/professional** posts. A written exam is common, and a practical test may be added for posts where it applies; the recruitment rules and notice determine the stages.

### General or administrative posts

Prepare the subjects named in the notice. Common areas include:

- Bangla and English language and grammar
- Mathematics and analytical reasoning
- General knowledge, Bangladesh affairs, and current affairs
- Computer/ICT basics
- Post-specific subjects such as administration, accounting, finance, economics, law, audit, or management

### Engineering and technical posts

Alongside any common language, math, or general-knowledge portion, study the **core subject for the post and degree**. Examples include civil, electrical/electronics, mechanical, chemical, petroleum/mining, computer engineering, geology, and geophysics. The relevant notice may also specify practical or applied questions.

**Do not assume one universal Petrobangla mark distribution.** Even in a combined recruitment, the paper can differ by post category. Confirm whether the exam is MCQ, written, or both, and whether there is a practical exam.

## BEPZA and EPZ-related recruitment

BEPZA's recruitment notices cover varied roles, including engineering, office/computer operations, surveying/drafting, trades, driving, and school or medical-center jobs. The selection process can therefore include a written exam, practical or skill test, demonstration, and/or viva depending on the post.

### General or office posts

Typical preparation areas may include Bangla, English, mathematics, general knowledge, ICT/computer operation, and post-related office or administrative knowledge. For computer operator roles, practise the stated typing, software, and practical requirements.

### Technical, trade, and specialist posts

Focus on the relevant diploma, trade, or professional subject. A practical test may assess hands-on skills for roles such as driver, electrician, operator, draftsman, or other trades. Teaching and medical roles may have professional subject assessments or demonstrations. The exact stages come from the particular notice.

## Other public, autonomous, and utility-sector employers

The same post-based approach is useful for organizations such as BPSC-recruited non-cadre posts, ministries and departments, power and energy companies, WASA, and other authorities or corporations.

| Post type | Main preparation focus |
|---|---|
| General administration / office | Bangla, English, math, general knowledge, ICT, and administration or office procedures |
| Accounts / audit / finance | Accounting, finance, audit, math, plus common language and general sections if listed |
| Engineering / technical | Degree or diploma subject, applied problem solving, and any common sections in the notice |
| Computer / IT | Programming, databases, networking, systems, cybersecurity or other role-specific topics, plus any common sections |
| Driver / operator / trade | Practical skill, safety, equipment, and relevant written basics if required |
| Teacher / health professional | Subject knowledge, professional knowledge, and possible demonstration or practical assessment |

## A simple way to prepare across sectors

1. **Read the exact circular and exam notice.** Write down the stages, subjects, marks, time, negative marking, and any practical test.
2. **Separate common topics from professional topics.** Keep a shared study routine for language, math, reasoning, and current affairs; add a separate block for your target post's discipline.
3. **Practise the format you will sit.** MCQ speed practice does not replace written answers, typing, practical tasks, or presentations.
4. **Use past papers carefully.** Treat them as examples of question style, not a guarantee that the next exam will use the same syllabus or marks.
5. **Track employer notices.** Exam stages and instructions can be updated after the original job circular.
